TIWA

A calm wellbeing companion for young mothers in the study.

TIWA helps adolescent girls who are pregnant or caring for a baby take part in a perinatal depression research study led with the University of Ibadan College of Medicine.

Read the privacy policy What TIWA is

TIWA is for enrolled study participants only. It is not a public consumer app and is not a substitute for emergency care.

Built for the study

On the phone, TIWA offers supportive videos, mood check-ins, clinic to-dos, and progress encouragement. Sensitive content such as voice notes and task text stays on the device. The app was developed by Loopbugs LTD for the research team.
